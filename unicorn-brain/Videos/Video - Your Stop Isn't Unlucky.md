## SHORT VERSION (Reels / TikTok / Shorts — ~35 sec)

**Format:** vertical, talking head + chart cutaways. Fast cuts, no dead air.

**[0:00–0:04] HOOK (talking head, direct)**
> "Your stop didn't get hit because you're unlucky. It got hit because it was exactly where everyone else's was."

**[0:04–0:12] Setup**
> "Look at any obvious high or low. Right above highs and right below lows is where most traders hide their stop losses. That's liquidity."

*(Visual: chart with a clean swing high, shade the zone above it, label "stops live here")*

**[0:12–0:24] The explanation**
> "Bigger players need those orders to fill their positions. So price runs that level first, takes the stops, and only then makes the real move."

*(Visual: price wicks above the high, then reverses hard — freeze frame on the wick, label "stop hunt")*

**[0:24–0:32] The reframe**
> "Once you see it like that, a stop hunt stops being bad luck and starts being information. The sweep is often where the setup begins — not where it ends."

*(Visual: same chart, arrow from the sweep into the reversal)*

**[0:32–0:36] Close**
> "So next time your stop gets taken, don't blame luck. Ask where everyone else's stop was. Your stop isn't unlucky. It's expected."

*(On-screen text: "Your stop isn't unlucky. It's expected.")*
