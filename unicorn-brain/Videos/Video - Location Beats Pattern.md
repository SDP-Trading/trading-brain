## SHORT VERSION (Reels / TikTok / Shorts — ~38 sec)

**Format:** vertical, talking head + split-screen charts.

**[0:00–0:04] HOOK (talking head)**
> "Your setup isn't bad. It's just in the wrong place, or at the wrong time."

*Alt hooks to test:* "Stop taking good setups in bad places." / "Where you enter matters more than what you enter."

**[0:04–0:10] The problem**
> "A setup can show up anywhere on your chart. In the middle of nowhere, it has little reason to work."

*(Visual: setup forming mid-range, label "no context")*

**[0:10–0:20] Place**
> "The same setup right after price taps a higher-timeframe Fair Value Gap, or sweeps a higher-timeframe high or low? That one has a reason to exist."

*(Visual: split screen — left: setup in the middle of nowhere; right: same setup after a HTF FVG tap / HTF sweep)*

**[0:20–0:27] Time**
> "And timing matters just as much. Around the session open is where the real moves start. Not in the dead hours."

*(Visual: vertical line at the session open, price expanding after it vs. flat chop midday)*

**[0:27–0:33] The rule**
> "The higher timeframe gives you the location. Time gives you the moment. The lower timeframe gives you the entry."

*(On-screen text, big: "RIGHT PLACE. RIGHT TIME. THEN ENTRY.")*

**[0:33–0:38] Close**
> "So stop chasing setups all day. Pick your spot, wait for your time, and let the trade come to you."

*Alt closes:* "The setup was never the problem. The when and where was." / "Same setup. Right place. Right time. That's the whole edge."
