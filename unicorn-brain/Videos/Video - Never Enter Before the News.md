## SHORT VERSION (Reels / TikTok / Shorts — ~33 sec)

**Format:** vertical, talking head + screen recording of the economic calendar.

**[0:00–0:04] HOOK (talking head)**
> "If there's big news today, this is the one rule I never break."

**[0:04–0:08] The rule**
> "I never enter before the release. Only after."

*(On-screen text, big: "AFTER. NEVER BEFORE.")*

**[0:08–0:16] Where to check**
> "Open an economic calendar like Forex Factory. The high-impact events — FOMC, rate decisions — are marked in red, with the exact time."

*(Visual: screen recording of Forex Factory, zoom on a red-folder event and its time)*

**[0:16–0:26] Why**
> "Before those releases, price tends to be indecisive. It chops around with no real direction. Entering there is basically betting on the number."

*(Visual: chart of choppy pre-news candles, then the release candle)*

**[0:26–0:33] Close**
> "After the release, the setups start making sense again. Waiting costs you nothing. Entering early can cost you the day."

*(On-screen text: "Red folder = wait.")*
