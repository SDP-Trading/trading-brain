## SHORT VERSION (Reels / TikTok / Shorts — 30-40 sec)

**Format:** vertical, talking head or voiceover over the chart. Fast cuts, no dead air.

**[0:00–0:05] HOOK (talking head, direct tone)**
> "Every chart goes through the same 3 phases. Once you notice it, you can't unsee it."

**[0:05–0:12] Setup**
> "It's called AMD. Or Power of Three. Accumulation, manipulation, distribution. That's it."

*(Visual: on-screen text stacking the three words as they're said — Accumulation → Manipulation → Distribution)*

**[0:12–0:28] The explanation (short, one phase at a time)**
> "First, price just sits there, ranging. Building up orders. That's accumulation.
> Then it fakes you out — grabs those orders. That's the manipulation.
> Then it actually moves, for real this time. That's distribution."

*(Visual: chart walkthrough, one phase per cut — a consolidation box, then a fake wick out one side, then the actual displacement in the opposite direction)*

**[0:28–0:34] The nuance**
> "Most people only watch for up or down. But the middle phase is the one that matters. Right after that fake-out, the real move starts."

*(Visual: highlight/zoom on the manipulation candle specifically, separate from the rest)*

**[0:34–0:40] Close / CTA**
> "So next time price is just ranging and confusing you — check which phase you're in. Not just which way it's going."

*(On-screen text: "Accumulation → Manipulation → Distribution" + CTA to your profile / indicator)*
