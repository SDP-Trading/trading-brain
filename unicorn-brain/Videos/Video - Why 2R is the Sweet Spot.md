## SHORT VERSION (Reels / TikTok / Shorts — 30-40 sec)

**Format:** vertical, talking head or voiceover over the chart. Fast cuts, no dead air.

**[0:00–0:05] HOOK (talking head, direct tone)**
> "People ask me this a lot — why do I always close out at 2R?"

**[0:05–0:14] Setup (cut to chart / diagram)**
> "Honestly, it's simple. I risk 1 to make 2, and that's pretty much it — I don't overthink it beyond that."

*(Visual: simple risk/reward diagram — 1R risk box below entry, 2R target box above it)*

**[0:14–0:28] The "why" (personal experience + the math)**
> "I used to chase bigger targets, hold trades longer — more stress, not more money. And when you break down the math, at 2R you only need to win around a third of your trades to be profitable."

*(Visual: talking head, then quick on-screen graphic — "2R target → only ~33% win rate needed to profit")*

**[0:28–0:35] The nuance**
> "If the move's really clean, sure, I'll let it run further into a draw on liquidity. But that's the exception, not the rule."

*(Visual: quick cut showing a draw-on-liquidity level further out, dimmed/secondary to the 2R box)*

**[0:35–0:40] Close / CTA**
> "It's not the exciting answer, but it's what's actually worked for me. If you're still changing your take-profit every trade, maybe give this a try."

*(On-screen text: "Risk 1 → Target 2. That's it." + CTA to your profile / indicator)*
