## SHORT VERSION (Reels / TikTok / Shorts — ~38 sec)

**Format:** vertical, talking head + chart cutaways.

**[0:00–0:04] HOOK (talking head)**
> "The move before the open is often the trap. The move after it is the trade."

**[0:04–0:13] The setup**
> "Before a session opens, price will sometimes build a clean high or low. It almost invites traders to position early."

*(Visual: pre-open price action forming a neat high, label "bait")*

**[0:13–0:24] The trap**
> "Then the open hits. Price runs straight through that level, takes every stop sitting there… and the real move starts."

*(Visual: vertical line at session open, price sweeps the high, then expands — label "liquidity taken at the open")*

**[0:24–0:32] Name it**
> "That's manipulation. Liquidity built on purpose, then taken right at the open."

*(Visual: the same chart with three labels: "built" → "taken" → "real move")*

**[0:32–0:38] Close / CTA**
> "So I don't chase the pre-open move. I wait for liquidity to be taken around the open — then I look for my entry."

*(On-screen text: "Watch the open, not the hour before it.")*
