# Video - Consistency in Trading

⬆ [[Video Ideas]]

**Source concept:** [[The Unicorn Model]]
**Status:** Scripted
**Format:** Short-form
**Language:** English

## SHORT VERSION (Reels / TikTok / Shorts — 30-40 sec)

**Format:** vertical, talking head. Fast cuts, no dead air.

**[0:00–0:05] HOOK (talking head, direct tone)**
> "Consistency doesn't mean winning every day. It means doing the same thing every day."

**[0:05–0:12] Setup**
> "Same checklist. Same rules. Same target. Every trade — win or lose."

*(Visual: on-screen checklist ticking off — Liquidity, Timing, HTF Context, Pattern)*

**[0:12–0:26] The explanation (personal)**
> "For me that's liquidity, timing, higher timeframe context, and the pattern itself. Same things, every single time. I'm not deciding it differently trade to trade."

*(Visual: quick chart cuts showing the same checklist applied to two different setups)*

**[0:26–0:32] The nuance**
> "Some days, that means no trade at all. And that's still consistency."

*(Visual: a "no trade" day — clean chart, no entries marked)*

**[0:32–0:38] Close / CTA**
> "So if you want to be consistent — stop chasing wins. Start chasing the same process, every time."

*(On-screen text: "Same process > every outcome." + CTA to your profile / indicator)*
