## SHORT VERSION (Reels / TikTok / Shorts — ~35 sec)

**Format:** vertical, talking head. Calm, honest tone — more storytelling than teaching.

**[0:00–0:04] HOOK (talking head)**
> "The most expensive trade you'll ever take is the one right after a loss."

**[0:04–0:10] The loss**
> "You take a loss. Fine. It's part of the game."

*(Visual: chart with a clean stop-out, red P&L number)*

**[0:10–0:18] The feeling**
> "But then you feel it. You need to make it back. Right now."

*(Visual: close-up, tense face / fast cuts)*

**[0:18–0:26] The mistake**
> "So you skip your checklist, size up, and jump into something that isn't even your setup. That's revenge trading."

*(On-screen text: "No checklist. Bigger size. Not your setup.")*

**[0:26–0:31] The cost + fix**
> "And it rarely costs you one more loss. It costs you the day. The fix is boring: one bad loss, close the chart, walk away."

**[0:31–0:35] Close**
> "The market will be there tomorrow. Make sure your account is too."

*(On-screen text: "The market will be there tomorrow.")*
