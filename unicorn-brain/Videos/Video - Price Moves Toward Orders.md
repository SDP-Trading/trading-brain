# Video - Price Moves Toward Orders

⬆ [[Video Ideas]]

**Source concept:** [[What is Liquidity]] · [[Liquidity Mapping]]
**Status:** Scripted
**Format:** Short-form (~35 sec)
**Language:** English

## SHORT VERSION (Reels / TikTok / Shorts — ~35 sec)

**Format:** vertical, talking head + chart cutaways.

**[0:00–0:04] HOOK (talking head)**
> "Price isn't random. It's going somewhere — and most of the time you can see where."

**[0:04–0:13] Buy-side vs sell-side**
> "Every obvious high has orders resting above it. That's buy-side liquidity. Every obvious low has orders resting below it. That's sell-side liquidity."

*(Visual: chart, mark highs in one color "BSL", lows in another "SSL")*

**[0:13–0:21] Why they stack there**
> "Stops, breakout entries, pending orders — they all pile up at the same levels, because everyone is looking at the same chart."

*(Visual: quick zoom into one level, stack of order icons appearing)*

**[0:21–0:31] Liquidity as a target**
> "Those pools act like magnets. Price often reaches for them first, and only then decides whether to continue or reverse."

*(Visual: price travels into a marked SSL pool, then reverses)*

**[0:31–0:37] Close / CTA**
> "So before asking 'up or down?', ask 'where are the orders waiting?' Follow for part two."

*(On-screen text: "Price moves toward orders.")*
