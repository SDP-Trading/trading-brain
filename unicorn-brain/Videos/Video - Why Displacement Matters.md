## SHORT VERSION (Reels / TikTok / Shorts — ~45 sec)

**Format:** vertical, talking head. Fast cuts, no dead air.

**[0:00–0:05] HOOK (talking head, direct tone)**
> "Displacement is the difference between a breaker worth trading and one you should skip."

**[0:05–0:12] Setup**
> "It's when price gets decisive. It pushes through a level instead of just grinding through it."

*(Visual: cut to chart — a strong, forceful move through a breaker area, single clean push)*

**[0:12–0:26] The explanation**
> "When that happens through the breaker, it usually leaves a Fair Value Gap behind. And that gap lining up with the breaker? That's literally what makes it a Unicorn — not just a breaker."

*(Visual: annotate the breaker + the FVG left behind by the displacement, then label "Unicorn")*

**[0:26–0:32] The nuance**
> "No displacement doesn't mean skip the trade. It just means treat it with more caution."

*(Visual: side-by-side — clean displacement vs. price grinding back through the breaker with no push)*

**[0:32–0:40] How to actually use this**
> "If you want to keep it mechanical, only take the clean displacement setups. If you're fine with more discretion, you can still take the weaker ones — just know you're accepting more risk on those."

*(Visual: two quick labels over the same side-by-side — "Mechanical: Scenario 1 only" / "Discretionary: both, sized differently")*

**[0:40–0:46] Close / CTA**
> "So before you enter, don't just look for the breaker. Look at how price got there."

*(On-screen text: "No displacement? No gap, no Unicorn." + CTA to your profile / indicator)*
