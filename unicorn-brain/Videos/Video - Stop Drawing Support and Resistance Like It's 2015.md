# Video - Stop Drawing Support and Resistance Like It's 2015

⬆ [[Video Ideas]]

**Source concept:** [[Smooth Edges as Draw on Liquidity]] · [[Smooth Edges and EQH-EQL]]
**Status:** Scripted
**Format:** Short-form (~36 sec)
**Language:** English

## SHORT VERSION (Reels / TikTok / Shorts — ~36 sec)

**Format:** vertical, talking head + chart cutaways. Slightly provocative tone.

**[0:00–0:04] HOOK (talking head)**
> "Stop drawing support and resistance like it's 2015."

**[0:04–0:12] What most traders see**
> "Equal highs. Equal lows. A perfectly smooth edge. Most traders look at that and see a wall price can't break."

*(Visual: chart with equal highs, someone draws a thick "resistance" line over them)*

**[0:12–0:20] What they do**
> "So they put their stops just above it, where it feels safe."

*(Visual: shade the area above the line, label "the safe room")*

**[0:20–0:30] The reframe**
> "But that safe room is exactly where the liquidity sits. The cleaner the level looks, the more orders are stacked behind it. That's why it so often gets run."

*(Visual: price spikes through the equal highs, wipes the shaded zone, then reverses)*

**[0:30–0:36] Close / CTA**
> "It's not a wall. It's a target. Next time, don't ask if it holds — ask when it gets taken."

*(On-screen text: "If it looks smooth, it's getting run.")*
