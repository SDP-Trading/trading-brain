# Smooth Edges as Draw on Liquidity

⬆ [[Smooth Edges and EQH-EQL]]

See also: [[How to Spot Smooth Edges]] · [[Liquidity Mapping]]

The concept of Smooth Edges revolves around liquidity. Retail traders often read this kind of price action as a signal, identifying a small zone above or below the highs/lows of the Smooth Edges where they perceive safety — trusting the horizontal barrier as a potential area of support or resistance.

Retail's "safe room" is our liquidity. Based on this, there's a predisposition for price to revisit these highs/lows to access the liquidity resting in those zones.

"If it looks smooth, you can bet your ass we are running through that." — ICT
