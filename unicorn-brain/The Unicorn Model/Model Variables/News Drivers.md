# News Drivers

⬆ [[Time]]

News drivers are scheduled economic events or announcements that can substantially influence market volatility. These are typically found on an economic calendar such as [forexfactory.com](https://forexfactory.com), highlighted with a red icon showing the topic, release time, and currency affected (e.g. Federal Funds Rate, FOMC Economic Projections, FOMC Statement).

On days with significant news drivers, we should focus on entering positions only **after** the news release, never before. Prior to the announcement, price tends to behave unpredictably and indecisively, leading to less favorable trading conditions.
