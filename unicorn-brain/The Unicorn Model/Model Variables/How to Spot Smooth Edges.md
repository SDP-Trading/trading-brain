# How to Spot Smooth Edges

⬆ [[Smooth Edges and EQH-EQL]]

See also: [[Smooth Edges as Draw on Liquidity]] · [[News Drivers]]

Smooth Edges typically form during consolidation phases, often associated with the original consolidations of an MMXM model.

An efficient place to look for them is the Asia range (20:00–00:00) — price tends to consolidate and build these smooth, equal highs/lows during the Asia session.

The best Smooth Edges are the ones easily recognizable at a glance, requiring minimal thought to spot. If you can't see it right away, it's probably not there.
