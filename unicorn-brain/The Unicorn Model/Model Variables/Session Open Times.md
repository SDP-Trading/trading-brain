# Session Open Times

⬆ [[Time]]

One of the primary time windows to anticipate volatility is during the opening times of trading sessions. We can expect significant market activity during four main trading sessions:

| Session | Time |
|---|---|
| London Session | 02:00 – 05:00 |
| NY AM Session | 09:30 – 12:00 |
| NY PM Session | 13:30 – 16:00 |
| Asia Session | 20:00 – 00:00 |

The Asia Session is typically low-volatility and range-bound (hence "range" in its name), so this model's main focus stays on the **London**, **New York AM**, and **New York PM** sessions.
