When we refer to time in trading, we mean specific windows during which key events occur. These windows matter because they align with periods of liquidity being injected into the market.

Unicorn patterns can appear across various timeframes and at any moment throughout the day, but not every pattern on the chart represents a viable setup worth pursuing — this is where time becomes significant. While multiple breakers may form throughout the day, their importance varies: the most significant breakers generally develop within specific time windows — session opening times and news drivers.

Unicorns, as an entry function, can technically emerge outside these windows, on any timeframe and pair — but success there is uncertain. When timing aligns with the model's focus windows, trade quality improves significantly.
