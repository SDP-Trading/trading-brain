## What is a Fair Value Gap

A Fair Value Gap, or FVG, is an area in price where the market moves aggressively and leaves behind an inefficient price range.

This usually happens during strong displacement, when price moves so quickly that one side of the market does not fully trade inside that area.

In simple terms: a Fair Value Gap is an imbalance created by strong price movement.
