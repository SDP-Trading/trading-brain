## Definition

A Breaker Block is one of the key components of the Unicorn entry model.

Before identifying a Breaker Block, we first need liquidity to be taken. This is important because the breaker depends on the [[Stop Hunt|stop hunt]] — in other words, we do not look for breakers randomly; we first identify which high or low has been taken, and then we locate the candle or group of candles that created the move into that liquidity.

A Breaker Block can be defined as:

- **Bullish Breaker Block**: the highest up-closed candle (if there is more than one, we use the consecutive up-closed candles) before the move that takes liquidity.
- **Bearish Breaker Block**: the lowest down-closed candle (if there is more than one, we use the consecutive down-closed candles) before the move that takes liquidity.

This means the breaker is the candle, or group of consecutive candles, that forms before price moves aggressively to take liquidity.
