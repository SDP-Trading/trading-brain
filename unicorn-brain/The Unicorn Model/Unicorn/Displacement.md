# Displacement

⬆ [[How to Qualify Good Breakers]]

When we want to consider a Breaker Block, we want price to encounter little to no rejection when approaching it. This means we want to observe price displacement that creates a [[Fair Value Gaps|Fair Value Gap]] along the way.

![[Displacement Scenarios.png]]

**Scenario 1** — price displaces through the Breaker Block with no resistance.

**Scenario 2** — price encounters resistance and shows little displacement, indicating a lack of decisive price action.

Does this mean we shouldn't take a trade based on Scenario 2? No — it simply means that when we observe displacement (Scenario 1), we can consider our breaker to be of higher quality, since price is clearly indicating its intention to move higher. If you prefer a mechanical approach, stick with Scenario 1 only. If you're open to some discretion, you can use Scenario 1 when it presents itself and decide whether to take a trade on a Scenario 2 breaker or not.
