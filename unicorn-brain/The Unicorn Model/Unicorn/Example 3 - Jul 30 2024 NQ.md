# Example 3 - Jul 30 2024 NQ

⬆ [[Trade Examples]]

- **Date:** Jul 30, 2024
- **Pair:** NQ
- **Session:** New York AM
- **Components:** [[Stop Hunt|Stop hunt]] · [[Displacement]] · Unicorn

In this example, the manipulation candle occurred precisely at 9:30, showing an initial upward move followed by an immediate reversal and displacement through a breaker.

Although the breaker and Fair Value Gap were not perfectly aligned, the FVG was used for the entry point, with the breaker serving as the invalidation level for added safety. The trade was targeted with a fixed 2R take profit.
