# SIBI and BISI

⬆ [[Fair Value Gaps]]

See also: [[Bullish Fair Value Gap]] · [[Bearish Fair Value Gap]] · [[Other Types of Gaps]]

SIBI and BISI are just a deeper way of naming what a Fair Value Gap represents for market price — the same FVG concept, described by which side got the inefficiency.

**SIBI — Sell-side Imbalance, Buy-side Inefficiency**
Also called a bullish Fair Value Gap. The price offered was on the buy-side, leaving behind a sell-side inefficiency.

**BISI — Buy-side Imbalance, Sell-side Inefficiency**
Also called a bearish Fair Value Gap. The price offered was on the sell-side, leaving behind a buy-side inefficiency.

Price does two things: seek liquidity, and rebalance. The inefficiencies left behind by price are likely to be revisited at some point as part of that rebalancing — which is exactly what we use to our advantage when trading FVGs.
