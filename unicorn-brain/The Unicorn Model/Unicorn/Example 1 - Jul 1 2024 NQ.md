# Example 1 - Jul 1 2024 NQ

⬆ [[Trade Examples]]

- **Date:** Jul 1, 2024
- **Pair:** NQ
- **Session:** New York AM
- **Components:** [[High Timeframe Context|Higher timeframe point of interest]] · [[Stop Hunt|Stop hunt]] · [[Displacement]] · Unicorn

Price reaches an hourly point of interest, marked by a 1-hour Swing High. After sweeping the higher timeframe (HTF) liquidity, there is a stop hunt followed by a distinct downward displacement.

Price leaves behind a breaker and a Fair Value Gap in alignment. At market open, price moves briefly in the opposite direction but eventually returns to move lower.
