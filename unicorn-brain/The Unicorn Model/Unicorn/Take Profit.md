# Take Profit

⬆ [[Unicorn]]

See also: [[Stop Loss Placement]] · [[Smooth Edges and EQH-EQL]] · [[Where to Enter]]

The general rule for take profit across all trades in this model is a **fixed 2R take profit**.

Following the practice of placing stop-loss orders under the breaker, trades often have a high probability of reaching the 2R mark, making it the sweet spot for profit.

Price can obviously run beyond 2R, so if you aim to capture larger moves, focus on draw on liquidity — particularly using [[Smooth Edges and EQH-EQL|Smooth Edges]] as your exit point for trades.
