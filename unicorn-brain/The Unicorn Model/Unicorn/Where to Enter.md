When it comes to execution, there are two ways to enter a trade:

- **Limit Orders** — waiting for the retest of the Breaker Block after the displacement.
- **Market Orders** — anticipating the retest and entering early to avoid missing the opportunity if a sharp move occurs.

A mix of both works well depending on how price is behaving. If you're new to the model, limit orders are recommended, since they remove the distraction and pressure of hitting the market button at the right moment. With time and experience, market orders tend to work best — they let you jump into a trade at the right time (or not) and avoid the frustration of a missed limit order.

## Where should we enter the trade?

We want to enter, or place our limit order, once we see a close above (or below, for shorts) the breaker after the displacement. The concept behind the Unicorn is that the breaker will support price as it continues its move — so we aim to enter at the breaker retest or, for a market order, as close to the breaker as possible.

See [[FVG Touch vs Breaker Touch Entry]] for the two price levels most commonly used for this.
