A higher-quality Breaker Block usually appears with strong [[Displacement|displacement]] — price moving aggressively through or away from the breaker area with little resistance, often creating a [[Fair Value Gaps|Fair Value Gap]] along the way.

Simple idea: **Breaker Block + Fair Value Gap aligned = Unicorn setup**
