# What is the Unicorn Pattern

⬆ [[Unicorn]]

See also: [[What is a Breaker Block]] · [[Fair Value Gaps]] · [[Where to Enter]]

The Unicorn is an entry function/pattern popularized by ICT within the 2022 mentorship. It can be used as a standalone trading pattern, but it's generally advised to combine it with additional variables — building a full model around the Unicorn function optimizes it beyond the basic pattern.

The Unicorn pattern consists of:

0. A **Lower Low (LL)**
1. A **Higher High (HH)**
2. A **Breaker Block**
3. A **Fair Value Gap (FVG)**, overlapping the established Breaker Block
4. A successful **re-test of the FVG + Breaker** that serves as the entry

A breaker block occurs when price breaks above or below an order block, signaling a shift in market direction. A Fair Value Gap occurs when there's an inefficiency/imbalance in the market — buying and selling not being equal — and it can act as a magnet for price before continuing in the same direction.

The merger of these two elements (breaker + FVG) is the foundation of the entry: we're harnessing market sentiment, bullish or bearish, as indicated by their alignment.

To confirm the move is likely to continue, we look for **displacement** through the breaker block — a significant, forceful price movement with intense buying or selling pressure — as a gauge of momentum before committing to the entry.
