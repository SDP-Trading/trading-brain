# Example 4 - Jul 25 2024 ES

⬆ [[Trade Examples]]

- **Date:** Jul 25, 2024
- **Pair:** ES
- **Session:** New York AM
- **Components:** [[High Timeframe Context|Higher timeframe point of interest]] · [[Stop Hunt|Stop hunt]] · [[Displacement]] · Unicorn · [[Smooth Edges and EQH-EQL|Smooth Edges]]

After a significant selloff following the session open, the key premise for this trade was the [[Smooth Edges and EQH-EQL|smooth edges]] that acted as the liquidity target.

Once price reached a higher timeframe level — specifically a 1-hour Swing Low — a brief pullback created a breaker. Following this displacement, a Unicorn formation emerged, providing an entry point with the target set at the smooth edges.
