# Example 2 - Jul 12 2024 NQ

⬆ [[Trade Examples]]

- **Date:** Jul 12, 2024
- **Pair:** NQ
- **Session:** New York AM
- **Components:** [[High Timeframe Context|Higher timeframe point of interest]] · [[Stop Hunt|Stop hunt]] · [[Displacement]] · Unicorn

In this scenario, we had a continuation pattern. At market open, price rallied, giving no entry opportunity initially.

Anticipating more upside, we observed manipulation into a higher timeframe PD array. Following the liquidity sweep, a significant displacement occurred, and price found support on the Unicorn formation (breaker + Fair Value Gap). This setup allowed for a trade entry, targeting the nearest high for a 2R trade.
