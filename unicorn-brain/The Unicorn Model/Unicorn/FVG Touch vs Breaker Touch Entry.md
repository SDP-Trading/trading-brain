# FVG Touch vs Breaker Touch Entry

⬆ [[Where to Enter]]

See also: [[What is the Unicorn Pattern]] · [[Stop Loss Placement]]

Beyond limit vs. market orders, there's a second decision to make: which price level to enter at. There are two primary methods, often recommended interchangeably depending on how price is behaving:

1. **Entry at the touch of the fully aligned FVG.**
2. **Entry at the touch of the Breaker Block.**

Neither is strictly better — a more adaptive approach, picking the method that fits the current market structure, tends to work better than mechanically using the same one every time.
