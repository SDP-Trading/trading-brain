# Stop Loss Placement

⬆ [[Unicorn]]

See also: [[Where to Enter]] · [[Take Profit]] · [[What is a Breaker Block]]

There are two ways to place a stop loss:

- Below/above the breaker.
- Below/above the high/low that swept liquidity.

The recommended approach is to place the stop loss beneath (or above, for shorts) the Breaker Block. The main premise of the model is that the breaker is what holds and supports price for the preceding move, so the point of invalidation should be the failure of the breaker.

Price can always wick through the breaker and still close beyond it — which is exactly why the second option (stop below the Lower Low / above the Higher High) exists: it's the safer placement when you want to give the trade room to survive a wick without invalidating on it.
