## What is High Timeframe Context

High Timeframe Context means using higher timeframe levels to understand where price is likely to react.

Instead of looking for Unicorns randomly, we want to search for them when price reaches an important higher timeframe area. The best areas to watch are usually:

- Higher timeframe Fair Value Gaps
- Higher timeframe Swing Highs
- Higher timeframe Swing Lows

These areas give us context because they represent important zones where price may seek liquidity, rebalance, or react.

## Why High Timeframe Context matters

A Unicorn by itself can appear anywhere on the chart, but not every Unicorn has the same quality. A Unicorn that forms in the middle of nowhere is usually weaker. A Unicorn that forms after price reaches a higher timeframe level is usually more meaningful, because it has a stronger reason to exist.

The higher timeframe gives us the location. The lower timeframe gives us the entry.

Higher Time Frame Points of Interest are one of the model's optional variables (see [[Model Variables]]) — they can strengthen a setup without being required for it. We focus on two PD arrays: Swing Highs/Lows and Fair Value Gaps.

We can leverage HTF Points of Interest by seeking Unicorn formations once these points are revisited — such as after sweeping a HTF swing high/low or tapping into a HTF Fair Value Gap. See [[HTF Fair Value Gaps]] and [[HTF Swing High-Low Liquidity]] for each in detail, and [[HTF to LTF Timeframe Alignment]] for which lower timeframe to watch once a HTF POI is revisited.
