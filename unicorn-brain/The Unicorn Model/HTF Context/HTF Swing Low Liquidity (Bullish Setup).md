# HTF Swing Low Liquidity (Bullish Setup)

⬆ [[HTF Swing High-Low Liquidity]]

See also: [[HTF Swing High Liquidity (Bearish Setup)]]

Simple sequence for a bullish setup:
1. Price sweeps a HTF swing low.
2. Sell-side liquidity is taken.
3. Price starts rejecting.
4. We drop to a lower timeframe.
5. We look for a Bullish Unicorn.
