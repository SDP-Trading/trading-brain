# HTF Swing High Liquidity (Bearish Setup)

⬆ [[HTF Swing High-Low Liquidity]]

See also: [[HTF Swing Low Liquidity (Bullish Setup)]]

Simple sequence for a bearish setup:
1. Price sweeps a HTF swing high.
2. Buy-side liquidity is taken.
3. Price starts rejecting.
4. We drop to a lower timeframe.
5. We look for a Bearish Unicorn.
