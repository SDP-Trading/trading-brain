# HTF Swing High / Low Liquidity

⬆ [[High Timeframe Context]]

## Subtopics
- [[HTF Swing High Liquidity (Bearish Setup)]]
- [[HTF Swing Low Liquidity (Bullish Setup)]]

See also: [[Swing High-Low Liquidity]] · [[Liquidity Mapping]]

Higher timeframe swing highs and swing lows are important because they usually hold liquidity.

A HTF swing high can contain buy-side liquidity. A HTF swing low can contain sell-side liquidity.

When price sweeps one of these levels, we do not automatically enter — the sweep only gives us the context. After the HTF liquidity is taken, we drop to a lower timeframe and look for a Unicorn entry model.

See [[HTF Swing High Liquidity (Bearish Setup)]] and [[HTF Swing Low Liquidity (Bullish Setup)]] for each sequence in detail.
