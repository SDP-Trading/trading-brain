## What are the model variables

The variables are the key elements or factors we need to consider before entering a trade. They help determine whether a trade is viable and assess the overall quality of the setup.

**Mandatory variables** — these must be present for a trade to align with the model's rules:
- [[Time]]
- [[Manipulation]]
- The Unicorn entry function itself: [[Stop Hunt]] → [[What is a Breaker Block|Breaker Block]] → [[How to Qualify Good Breakers|Displacement]] → [[Fair Value Gaps]]

**Optional variables** — not required, but their presence boosts confidence and adds quality to the trade:
- [[Smooth Edges and EQH-EQL|Smooth Edges or Equal Highs/Lows]]
- Higher timeframe point of interest — see [[High Timeframe Context]]

To finish applying the model, see [[Where to Enter]], [[Stop Loss Placement]] and [[Take Profit]] for suggested entry, stop-loss and take-profit rules, and [[Trade Examples]] for worked examples from recent price action.
