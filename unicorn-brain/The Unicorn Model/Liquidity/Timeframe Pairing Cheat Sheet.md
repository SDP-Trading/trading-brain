# Timeframe Pairing Cheat Sheet

⬆ [[Liquidity Mapping]]

See also: [[HTF to LTF Timeframe Alignment]]

- 15-minute or 1-hour liquidity → 1-minute entries
- 1-hour or 4-hour liquidity → 5-minute entries
- 1-hour or 4-hour liquidity → 15-minute entries
- 4-hour or Daily liquidity → 1-hour entries

These are standard mappings that can help create consistency in your trading process.

The logic is simple: the higher timeframe identifies where liquidity is being targeted, and the lower timeframe helps us find a clean entry once price reacts from that liquidity area.

You can also create your own timeframe mappings by following the same principle: higher timeframe liquidity for context, lower timeframe execution for entry.
