# OHLC Sweep

⬆ [[OHLC Liquidity]]

See also: [[OLHC Sweep]]

OHLC liquidity happens when a higher timeframe candle opens, trades above the high of the previous higher timeframe candle, and then moves to the opposite side.

In simple terms:

**Open → High → Low → Close**

Price opens, takes the previous candle's high, and then moves lower. This means buy-side liquidity was taken first.

After that, we can start looking for bearish opportunities on a lower timeframe.
