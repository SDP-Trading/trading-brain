# Swing Low Liquidity

⬆ [[Swing High-Low Liquidity]]

See also: [[Swing High Liquidity]]

Swing low liquidity (sell-side) is found below previous swing lows.

When price forms a low and then moves higher, traders who are long may place their stop losses below that low. At the same time, breakout traders may place sell orders below the same level, expecting price to continue lower if the low is broken. This creates a pool of sell-side liquidity below the swing low.

> Swing low liquidity = orders resting below a previous low.

When price trades below that low, it is said to be **taking** or **sweeping** sell-side liquidity.
