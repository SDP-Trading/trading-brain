# OLHC Sweep

⬆ [[OHLC Liquidity]]

See also: [[OHLC Sweep]]

OLHC liquidity happens when a higher timeframe candle opens, trades below the low of the previous higher timeframe candle, and then moves to the opposite side.

In simple terms:

**Open → Low → High → Close**

Price opens, takes the previous candle's low, and then moves higher. This means sell-side liquidity was taken first.

After that, we can start looking for bullish opportunities on a lower timeframe.
