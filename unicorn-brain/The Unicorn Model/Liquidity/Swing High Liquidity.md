# Swing High Liquidity

⬆ [[Swing High-Low Liquidity]]

See also: [[Swing Low Liquidity]]

Swing high liquidity (buy-side) is found above previous swing highs.

When price forms a high and then moves lower, traders who are short may place their stop losses above that high. At the same time, breakout traders may place buy orders above the same level, expecting price to continue higher if the high is broken. This creates a pool of buy-side liquidity above the swing high.

> Swing high liquidity = orders resting above a previous high.

When price trades above that high, it is said to be **taking** or **sweeping** buy-side liquidity.
