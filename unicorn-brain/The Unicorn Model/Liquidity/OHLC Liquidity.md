OHLC and OLHC liquidity describes how a higher timeframe candle delivers price after opening.

The idea is simple: once a new higher timeframe candle opens, price may first move to take liquidity from the previous higher timeframe candle before moving in the opposite direction.

This helps us understand which side of liquidity price targeted first.

## How to use it

1. Identify the previous higher timeframe candle.
2. Mark its high and low.
3. When the new candle opens, observe whether price takes the previous high or the previous low first.
4. If price takes the previous high and rejects, look for bearish entries on a lower timeframe.
5. If price takes the previous low and rejects, look for bullish entries on a lower timeframe.

See [[OHLC Sweep]] and [[OLHC Sweep]] for each pattern in detail.
