   b) Pairing a higher-timeframe liquidity area with a lower-timeframe entry model

   b) 5-minute or 15-minute

   d) Weekly
4. True or False: if price sweeps a previous swing low, you should look for bearish confirmation on a lower timeframe.
5. True or False: taking liquidity alone is enough reason to enter a trade, without needing a lower-timeframe entry model.
6. An "OHLC sweep" (Open → High → Low → Close) tells you:
   a) Sell-side liquidity was taken first, so look for bullish setups
   b) Buy-side liquidity was taken first, so look for bearish setups

>[!question]- Answer Key
> 1. b
> 2. b
> 3. b

> 6. b
